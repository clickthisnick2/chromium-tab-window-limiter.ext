While the limiter is on, opening an unpinned tab past 10 closes that new tab, and opening another normal window closes that new window. Incognito windows close immediately. Pinned tabs, popups, and DevTools stay open. Pause lasts 10 seconds, then the limiter turns back on. The All windows count matches the toolbar badge.
